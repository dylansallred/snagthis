How it works

Three moves. No manual.

  1. 01

    Play the video

    Open a page and start the video. SnagThis notices what's playing.

  2. 02

    Click SnagThis

    The extension lists the videos it found. Pick quality, audio and subtitles.

  3. 03

    Press save

    Plain video files go straight to Chrome Downloads. Streams go to the desktop app.

Got the desktop app? Pair it with one click in the extension. They share your settings and accent colour.

Privacy

Everything stays on your computer.

  • No account

    Nothing to sign up for, no paid tier.

  • No tracking

    No telemetry, no analytics. This site has no trackers either.

  • Local by design

    Downloads, processing, your library and settings live on your device.

  • Private pairing

    The extension only talks to the app on your own computer, with its own token. Disconnect from either side.

  • Direct connections

    SnagThis talks to the video's site, plus lookups and update checks you control.

  • Your call on support

    Diagnostics are redacted, and only shared if you choose to.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. SnagThis is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 licence. There's no account and no paid tier. The code is on GitHub.

Do I need the desktop app?

No. The extension saves MP4 and WebM files straight to Chrome Downloads on its own. Add the app when you want streamed video, separate audio and subtitle tracks, or a Saved library.

How do the extension and app connect?

With the app open, click to pair in the extension. That's it. Each browser gets its own private connection, and either side can disconnect.

Why won't it save some videos?

Some sites lock their video with DRM so it only plays in their own player. SnagThis respects that and never records, unlocks or saves protected video. Other videos on the page that aren't protected can still be saved.

What can I use it for?

Videos you own or have permission to save: your own uploads, openly licensed films, classes and talks you're allowed to keep. Check each site's terms.

Which systems does it run on?

The extension runs in Chrome on your computer. The desktop app is available for Mac and Windows.
