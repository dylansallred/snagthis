W · Wordmark

Should the letters be pixelated too? Every typeface is scaled so its capitals are exactly the button’s height (cyan guides: capital line and baseline). Each card shows the lockup large on dark and white, then at the real 22 px top bar size, which is where readability is decided. The three pixel fonts in W4–W6 were picked from Pixelify Sans, Tiny5, Jersey 10/15, Micro 5, DotGothic16, VT323, Doto and others as the ones that stay readable at 22 px.
