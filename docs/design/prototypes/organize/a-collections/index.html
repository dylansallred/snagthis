A slim Collections sidebar beside the list. Collections are virtual: a video can sit in several at once, and nothing moves on disk. A list header above the rows names the place, its size, and the current sort. Drag rows onto a collection, or use ⋯ ▸ Add to collection… for one or many selected videos.
