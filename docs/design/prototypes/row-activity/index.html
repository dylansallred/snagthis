Showing live download activity on a collapsed row.

Each option adds one small, live treatment at the right of a collapsed downloading row, beside Pause, echoing what Details shows when the row is open. Speeds and progress are simulated and respond to the slider below. Every option keeps the 40-cell lane, leaves the thumbnail clear and keeps one visible action. Click an option or press 1–9, 0, - (or ← →). Hover a row to see the Soft focus state.

In a list fast · slow (with stalls) · finishing · paused · waiting · saved. Click Pause or Resume on any row.
