# UI refresh studies

[`folder.html`](folder.html) compares **six folder-bar hover treatments**, **four folder animations**, and **twelve copied confirmations** in the desktop details card. These choices combine independently. Alive matches the existing row-folder animation; Still and Original preserve the previous baseline. Five new copy options use sheets, a clipboard, link echoes, a floating label, or a light sweep instead of a checkmark. The preview includes normal/long paths, 820px/640px widths, held hover, reduced motion, and URL-persisted selections. Folder and copy clicks simulate feedback without opening files or changing the clipboard. The owner selected **Soft reveal + Open + settle** for the desktop folder bar, plus **Sheets snap** for copy feedback, all now applied. Other choices remain comparisons.

[`transfer.html`](transfer.html) explores speed-graph backdrops behind the desktop segment map. The owner chose **Outlined retries**, now applied in the desktop, and retained the current segment palette and graph position. The current round keeps the filled graph and provides independent color pickers and opacity sliders for its fill and line, plus opacity sliders for pending, downloading, and retrying squares. Completed cells remain fully visible. Defaults match the current desktop graph colors and fill opacity. Use the progress simulation to compare the treatments. The owner selected fill **#FA5D0E / 85%**, line **#FF7566 / 100%**, pending **25%**, downloading **100%**, retrying **75%**, and completed **100%**. This combination is applied in the desktop and is the study default; the legend remains fully visible. Further adjustments remain exploratory.

Three directions for refreshing the desktop window and the Chrome popup together: **A · Refined** (same layout, sharper details), **B · Spotlight** (active downloads in a card, quiet saved list, hero popup for one video) and **C · Gallery** (saved videos as a poster grid, docked details). The owner selected **A · Refined**, now implemented in both apps; B and C remain comparison studies.

`hover.html` is a follow-up study: nine row hover/focus treatments (round three: variations and combinations of Focus and Frame, the owner's favourites from round two) on design A for both surfaces. Pick one with the buttons, or keep the pointer on a row and press 1–9. The owner chose **5 · Focus mono**, now implemented in both apps.

Open `index.html` directly or serve the repository and open `/docs/design/prototypes/ui-refresh/`. The controls switch the download state, progress and how many videos the popup finds. Click rows or posters for details, hover thumbnails for the muted excerpt, and press Download in the popup. Each design lists what changes and where it departs from `ui-design-spec.md`.

All three keep the shared dark tokens, clean hover/focus thumbnails, the Pieces lane and one visible action per row. These studies originally used Blender open-film excerpts; the app now uses the original Pixel worlds clips in `apps/extension/popup/media/` (see that directory's `CREDITS.md`). No downloader APIs or user files are touched.

`edge.html` follows Focus mono into production: the 2px open-row edge and the 3px hover edge clash when an open row is hovered, and the owner wants hover to animate on and off more smoothly. It offers four open-row edge behaviours (A–D, plus Today) and five hover animations (1–5) that combine freely. The owner chose **C · Grey when open** with **2 · Grow**, now implemented in both apps.

`surface.html` studies the desktop list background and row separation after the owner found rows hard to tell apart and disliked the inner background: six separation styles (1–6) and five background palettes (A–E) combine freely, with the shipped Focus mono hover. The owner chose **Lines** for separation; the page now fixes Lines and offers twelve background palettes (A–L) and eight header/footer treatments (1–8). The owner then chose the **Orange line** bars and asked for more shades near Today, Deep black and Graphite: the page now fixes Lines and Orange line and offers six tints (A–F) by seven depths (1–7), with a readout of the exact colours. The owner chose **E4 · Steel, Dark**, then revised it to **E3 · Steel, Very dark**; Lines, Orange line and that palette are implemented in both apps.

`studies.html` holds three further studies on the shipped look: eight download-progress animations, nine Download button styles (several animated to invite a click, all with press feedback) and six layouts for the desktop expanded section. Round two followed the owner's feedback: eight variations on Pieces wave and pop, nine on the pulsing and outline-fill buttons, and six new expanded-section directions (poster, inspector card, tabs, speed graph, side drawer, film strip). The owner chose **Spark** for progress (implemented in both apps). Round three explores outline/breathing button combinations and live speed-graph, inspector-card and compact or ambient poster details, including hybrids. The owner chose **Outline + breathing glow** for the Download button (implemented in the popup) and **Graph in a card** for the expanded section; round four offers eight variations on that card. The owner then picked **Instrument** with a filled area and no numbers or grid; round five offers nine variations of that chart (tone, smoothing, density, stepped, low horizon, tile). The owner picked the **Orange** instrument chart; round six offers five ways to keep the facts and Pieces count readable where the chart passes behind them. The owner chose **Ceiling + halo** (being implemented with the inspector card). Round seven compares nine ways the Download button's hover fill can arrive, because the shipped bottom-up rise shows a square edge. The inspector card with the orange chart is implemented in the desktop app. Round eight compares eight layouts for the speed and connections readout (peak removed at the owner's request), keeps the nine hover-fill options, and adds a switch to preview other names for "Pieces". The owner chose **Liquid**, **Connection dots** and the name **Segments**; all three are implemented.

`motion.html` studies two desktop questions: eight ways the details card can open and close (today it appears instantly), and nine looks and hover/selection motions for the All · Downloading · Saved tabs. The owner chose **Drawer** and **Follow hover**; both are implemented.

`controls.html` holds four more studies: hover colour and copy feedback for Copy link / Open page (replacing the toast), hover colour and motion for the row's folder, more and play icons, six designs for the Remove dialog (each keeps list removal and Trash clearly apart with the safe choice first), and five Settings designs shown for the desktop sheet and the Chrome popup together (the popup variants replace its empty Advanced accordion with a single link into the app). The owner selected **Underline**, **Alive**, **Popover**, and **Grouped cards**, now applied in the desktop app and extension. Other choices remain comparisons.

`metadata.html` explores the Quality / Size / From facts and the saved-row status line: seven quality styles, six details layouts, and six saved-line layouts can be combined independently. Shared controls switch quality and preview width. Samples keep the exact resolution visible alongside any tier label: 480p / SD, 720p / HD, 1080p / Full HD, and 2160p / 4K; unknown quality stays unknown. The owner selected **Solid badge**, **Inset tiles**, and **Quality first**, now applied to the relevant desktop and extension metadata. The study opens with this combination; the other options remain comparisons.

[`thumbnails.html`](thumbnails.html) compares six loading placeholders and five thumbnail layouts, independently selectable in a 480px Chrome popup and desktop rows. The owner selected **Three dots** and **Flush full height** for both surfaces; the study opens on that combination. Switch between placeholders and loaded images to compare framing; pause motion or preview reduced motion. The URL preserves the combination, and Reset to original restores the previous still stripes and inset frame. Other treatments remain comparison studies.

[`logo-entrance.html`](logo-entrance.html) explores app-opening motion with the untouched original logo: Current, Soft rise, Light wipe, Arrow leads, Word build, and Shutters. Choose the reveal independently from its placement (in the header or centered before docking) and pace. Hover the numbered choices for quick previews; click to replay the full opening. Reduced motion is supported, and the URL preserves the combination. The owner selected **Arrow leads · Center → header · Natural**, now used at desktop startup. The final shimmer is followed by a shortened 400ms hold; total opening time is 3.1 seconds. Other choices remain previews.
