How it works

Three steps. No account.

  1. 01

    Open a page with a video

    SnagThis quietly spots the videos playing on it. The toolbar icon shows how many.

  2. 02

    Pick what you want

    Choose the quality, an audio track or subtitles. Sizes are shown up front.

  3. 03

    Press Snag

    Simple files go straight to Chrome Downloads. Streams go to the desktop app.

Features

Small tool. Careful details.

  • Pick what you want

    Quality, audio and subtitles, with the size shown first.

  • Close the popup

    Downloads keep going. Reopen it any time to check in.

  • Streams too

    HLS and DASH, with separate audio and subtitles joined for you.

  • Pause and resume

    Pieces already saved stay saved.

  • Pairs in one click

    Check four digits match, choose Allow. No account.

  • Your colour, everywhere

    Pick an accent once. The app and the extension match.

Privacy

Your videos stay on your computer.

Downloads and processing happen on your device. Requests go straight to the site the video comes from. There’s no SnagThis server in the middle.

FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No paid tier, no account. SnagThis is open source under the GPL-3.0 licence.

Do I need the desktop app?

Not for simple video files. The extension saves direct MP4 and WebM files to Chrome Downloads on its own. Streams, separate audio or subtitles, and the Saved library need the app.

Why can’t it save some videos?

Some sites lock their videos with DRM so they only play inside their own player. SnagThis respects that and never records or unlocks protected video. Other videos on the same page are still listed.

What am I allowed to save?

Videos you own or have permission to save. Check the site’s terms and the creator’s licence.

How do the app and the extension connect?

Choose Connect in the extension. The app shows four digits; if they match the ones in Chrome, choose Allow. They talk only to each other on your computer, and either side can disconnect at any time.

Where do my files go?

Extension-only saves go to your Chrome Downloads folder. The app saves to Downloads/SnagThis by default; you can pick another folder in its settings.
