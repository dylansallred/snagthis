# Sample media

These clips and posters are original pixel-art animations made for SnagThis: *Neon Rain*, *Ember Tide*, *Star Courier* and *Sky Hop*. Every frame and the chiptune soundtracks are generated by code, with no third-party images, footage or music. They are part of SnagThis and share its license (GPL-3.0-only).

The generators live in `docs/design/prototypes/sample-media/1-pixel/` (`generate.mjs` for the video, `src/soundtrack.mjs` for the audio).
