SnagThis test clips

This page exists so you can try the SnagThis Chrome extension on real files. Both clips are ten-second original pixel-art animations made for SnagThis. Play one, then click the SnagThis icon in the Chrome toolbar.

Star Courier

MP4 (H.264 and AAC), 1280×720, 10 seconds, about 0.7 MB.

Direct link to star-courier.mp4

Sky Hop

WebM (VP9 and Opus), 640×360, 10 seconds, about 0.35 MB.

Direct link to sky-hop.webm

What to do

  1. Press play on either clip.
  2. Click the SnagThis icon in the Chrome toolbar (pin it from the Extensions menu first if needed). Both clips are listed with their quality, size and length.
  3. Click the download button on a row. Chrome saves the file to your usual Downloads folder, and it appears in Chrome Downloads.

No account, sign-in or desktop app is needed for these files.

SnagThis is for videos you own or have permission to save. DRM-protected video is always refused.
